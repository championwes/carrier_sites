About JBD Transit

A carrier you can count on

Based in Arab, Alabama, JBD Transit LLC is a for-hire interstate motor carrier registered with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration under USDOT 3529027 (MC-1174187). We specialize in flatbed and open-deck freight — the steel, lumber, oilfield equipment and building materials that keep industry moving.

We're a focused, owner-operated fleet, not a faceless mega-carrier. That means when you call, you reach people who know your freight, and your load gets the attention it deserves from pickup to delivery.
